Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

In short
  • No account, no cookies, no ads, no selling of data.
  • Your location stays on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • To show the map and the live data, your browser contacts a few external services, which see your IP address. They are listed below.
  • Visits are counted anonymously with GoatCounter, without cookies. You can switch this off in the app (Info panel).

2. What happens when you use the app

Hosting (GitHub Pages)

The app is delivered by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). To deliver the pages and protect the service, GitHub processes your IP address and technical data such as browser type and time of access in its server logs. The app also loads the daily occupancy statistics and the charger list from raw.githubusercontent.com (also GitHub). See GitHub's privacy statement.

Program libraries (cdnjs)

The map libraries Leaflet and MapLibre are loaded from cdnjs, a service of Cloudflare, Inc. (USA). Cloudflare receives your IP address and technical request data. The files are checked with integrity hashes so they cannot be altered unnoticed.

Map (OpenFreeMap)

Map tiles come from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org). OpenFreeMap receives your IP address and which part of the map is being loaded. The map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors and © OpenMapTiles.

Live garage counts and EV charging status

Your browser loads the free spaces directly from the website of the City of Lugano (www.lugano.ch) and the charging status from the Swiss federal geodata portal (data.geo.admin.ch). Both receive your IP address and technical request data, as with any website visit.

Place search (Photon)

When you type in the search field of “Going to”, the text you type is sent, together with your IP address, to the Photon search service of komoot GmbH (Germany) to find matching places. Please do not type personal information into the search field.

Your location

The app uses your location only if you tap the location button or sort by distance, and only after your browser has asked for your permission. The location is processed only on your device, to sort the garages and show the parking zone you are in. It is not sent to us or to any third party. If you use “Navigate”, “Open in Maps” or “Find car”, Google Maps opens with the coordinates of the garage or your parked car; from then on Google's privacy policy applies.

Data stored on your device

Settings such as language, favourites or where you parked are stored only in your browser (local storage). Details and how to delete them: Cookies & storage.

Visit count (GoatCounter)

To know how many people use the app, page views are counted with GoatCounter (operated by Martin Tournoij, Ireland; servers of Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany and Finland). GoatCounter stores nothing on your device and does not store your IP address. It keeps aggregated data: the page or tab viewed, the referring website, browser and operating system, language, screen width and the country derived from the IP address. No personal profile is created. If your browser sends “Do Not Track” or “Global Privacy Control”, nothing is counted. You can also switch counting off in the app's Info panel. See GoatCounter's privacy policy.

Parking disc reminder

If you allow notifications, the reminder 10 minutes before your disc runs out is created on your device. No push server is involved.

Feedback by email

The feedback button opens your email app with a prepared message. It contains the app version, the open view, the language and your browser's technical identification, which you can delete before sending. If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and delete them when the matter is settled, at the latest after two years.

Space reports

For garages without live data, the app can let drivers report “I parked here, there was space”. When this feature is switched on, only the garage and the time are stored in a database of Supabase Inc. (hosted in Switzerland or the EU) and deleted after 7 days. No account, no location and no device identifier are stored; Supabase processes your IP address to deliver the request.

3. Purposes and legal basis

We process data only to provide the app, keep it secure, answer messages and understand how much it is used. As far as the EU General Data Protection Regulation applies, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in offering a working and secure free service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and, for your location and notifications, your consent given in the browser (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time in your browser settings.

4. Disclosure abroad

Some of the providers above are located or process data outside Switzerland: in the EU (Germany, Finland, Ireland), which Switzerland recognises as providing adequate data protection, and in the USA (GitHub, Cloudflare, and Google when you open Google Maps). These US companies state that they are certified under the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which the Federal Council recognises as adequate since 15 September 2024.

5. How long data is kept

We do not run our own server and do not keep logs. Emails are kept as described above. The external providers keep their technical logs according to their own policies, usually for a short time. Data on your device stays there until you delete it.

6. Your rights

You can ask for information about the personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to processing and, where applicable, receive a copy. Write to juliancedric.bigler@student.unisg.ch. You can also contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or, if you live in the EU, the data protection authority of your country.

7. Changes

We update this policy when the app changes. The current version is always published on this page.
