1. Scope

These terms apply to the use of Lugano Parking at luganoparking.ch (the “app”), operated by Julian Bigler, Hettiswil, Switzerland (see Legal notice). By using the app you accept these terms.

2. What the app is

The app shows free spaces in Lugano's car parks, helps choose a car park for a destination and explains the street parking rules. It is a free, independent, private project. It is not an official service of the City of Lugano, the car park operators, SBB, TPL or the clubs whose games are listed, and it is not endorsed by them.

3. Information without guarantee

The data comes from third parties (City of Lugano real-time feed, posted tariffs, the federal charging register, OpenStreetMap) and can be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Walking times, costs, trends (“full in about 20 min”), typical occupancy, event warnings and zone outlines are estimates. The signs at the car park and at the parking bay, and the official regulations, always apply. The parking disc helper is a reminder only: you remain responsible for setting your disc correctly and for respecting the rules.

4. Use while driving

Do not operate the app while driving. Set your destination before you leave or let a passenger use it.

5. Liability

The app is provided free of charge and “as is”, without any guarantee of availability or accuracy. As far as the law allows, the operator is not liable for damage resulting from the use of the app or of its information, in particular parking fines, towing costs, waiting times or a full car park. Liability for intent and gross negligence remains unaffected.

6. Fair use

Use the app only for its purpose. Do not send false space reports, do not try to overload or attack the app or the services it uses, and do not copy its data in bulk for commercial purposes.

7. Prices, payments and refunds

The app is free. There is nothing to buy in the app, so no payments are taken and no refunds apply. Parking fees are paid directly to the car park operators or the city, under their own conditions and refund rules. Should paid features ever be offered, their price and refund terms will be shown clearly before any purchase.

8. Sponsors
